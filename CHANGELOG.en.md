# Release notes

English version of CHANGELOG.md, shown in the app when the interface is in English. Same headings, same version numbers.

## 0.5.0

Onde speaks English, and comes to DaVinci Resolve.

### What's new
- **English interface**: Onde follows your computer's language. You can change it in Settings → Appearance → Language (Français, English, or like the system). In English, sounds, their descriptions and categories are shown in English too.
- **DaVinci Resolve Studio panel**: search, listen and place sounds without leaving Resolve. **Insert** places the sound at the playhead on a free audio track, and files it in an "Onde" bin. You can also drag a sound to the timeline or the Media Pool. One-click install from the Integrations button, then Workspace → Workflow Integrations → Onde. The panel requires the Studio version of Resolve: with the free version, dragging from the app works as before.
- The Premiere panel speaks English too when Premiere is in English.

## 0.4.0

Your own sounds in Onde, playlists and clearer navigation.

### What's new
- **Your own sounds**: add your folders in Settings → Sound folder → My sounds. They show up in "My sounds", in search and in playlists, next to the library. Supported formats: WAV, AIFF, MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A, CAF. Files stay where they are: Onde never copies, moves or deletes them.
- **Playlists**: under Favorites, "Liked sounds" and your playlists ("Library #01", "Library #02"… and you can rename them). Add library sounds and your own sounds with "Add to a playlist" in the right panel.
- **A star on every sound** in the list, to like a sound without leaving the list.
- **Tidier categories**: the category list folds away in the left sidebar, and a drop-down menu replaces the row of buttons above the results.

## 0.3.2

Offline mode, automatic sound cleanup and several fixes.

### What's new
- **Offline mode**: without a connection, sounds you've already played stay available, for listening and drag-and-drop. A bar shows how many sounds are on your computer, and you can still show the whole library if needed.
- **Automatic cleanup**: sounds you only listened to are deleted after 30 days without being played, so your disk doesn't fill up. Adjustable in Settings → Sound folder (7, 30, 90 days or never). Sounds dragged into an editing app, from the app or the Premiere panel, are never deleted: your projects always find them.
- **Onde reopens the way you left it**: in compact mode or as a large window.
- **New icons**: a gear for Settings, a sun and a moon to switch between light and dark mode, and an icon for compact mode.

### Fixes
- The list no longer jumps back to the top when you play a sound further down.
- The launch animation sound goes back to the previous version, which doesn't clip.
- Mac: Onde opens in front instead of starting behind other windows.
- Mac: the mode setting says "Like macOS" instead of "Like Windows".

## 0.3.1

New address: ondesound.com.

- The website moves to **ondesound.com**; the old address redirects there automatically.
- A link to the website in Settings → About.

## 0.3.0

Onde comes to Mac, with a new launch animation.

- **Mac version** (Intel and Apple Silicon, macOS 11 and later).
- Logo animation every time Onde opens, with sound (adjustable: with sound, without sound, off).
- **Premiere Pro panel** (2019 and later): search, listen and insert without leaving Premiere, installed in one click from the app.
- Animated transition between the welcome screen and the app.
