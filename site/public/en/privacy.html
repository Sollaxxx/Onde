Privacy

Updated: November 21, 2026

Onde collects the bare minimum: your email address if you give it to join the beta, and the feedback you choose to send from the app.

What we collect

  • Your email address, the date you signed up and the language of the page, through the “Join the beta” form.
  • If you send feedback from the app: your message, its type (bug, idea…), the Onde version and the system (for example “windows x86_64”), and your email address only if you add it so we can reply.
  • If you submit a sound from the app (once this feature opens): the file, its title, category, tags and description, the nickname you choose (published with the sound), your email address (never published, used to contact you about your sound) and a one-way fingerprint of your IP address, used only to limit abusive submissions.
  • If you create an Onde account (to join a team with Onde Team): your email address, the name you choose to show your team (optional), the teams you belong to and your role, the invitations you send and receive, and for each signed-in device, the sign-in date and a simple description (for example “Onde 0.5.0 · windows x86_64”). The sign-in code sent by email and the session token are only stored as one-way fingerprints.
  • Nothing else: no phone number, no ad tracking. An account is never needed to use the free library.

Why

Only to let you know about Onde's major milestones (beta, macOS version, public launch), and to fix and improve Onde thanks to your feedback. Your data is never sold, rented or used for advertising.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by submitting the form and can withdraw at any time. For Onde Team accounts: performance of the service you or your studio asked for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

How long

Until you unsubscribe, and at the latest six months after Onde's public launch. After that, your address is deleted. Feedback sent from the app is kept for two years at most. An Onde account is kept until you delete it, which you can do yourself in Onde (Settings, Account); sessions expire after six months and sign-in codes after ten minutes.

Where the data is stored

In a database hosted by Cloudflare, Inc., acting as a processor. Data may be processed in the United States; Cloudflare covers these transfers under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Sign-in and invitation emails are sent by Resend, Inc., acting as a processor, which only receives the recipient's address and the email's content. These transfers are covered in the same way.

Cookies and analytics

The site sets no cookies. Traffic may be measured with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which works without cookies and without identifying visitors. Your light or dark mode and language choices stay in your browser and are never sent.

Fonts and scripts are hosted by the site itself: your visit sends no requests to Google or any other third party.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your data, and withdraw your consent. Write to contact.ondesound@gmail.com: requests are handled within one month.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority: cnil.fr.
